What is auto advertising

Auto ads, moved
inside the auto.

Auto advertising in Hyderabad has usually meant a printed panel on the back hood of an auto-rickshaw, seen for a few seconds by the traffic behind it. Digital auto advertising moves your ad inside, onto a screen that faces the passenger for the length of the trip.

That makes auto ads useful for local businesses: the passenger is often travelling through the very streets your shop, clinic or coaching centre is on. Commute Ads books the screens with our auto partners, reviews your ad before it runs, and sends you photos from the autos showing it on screen.

Auto advertising FAQs.

What is auto advertising?

Auto advertising means placing your brand on auto-rickshaws. Traditionally that is a printed panel on the back hood. Commute Ads runs digital auto ads instead: your ad plays on a screen inside the auto, facing the passenger.

How much do auto ads cost in Hyderabad?

It depends on how many autos, which areas and how long your campaign runs. Send us an auto advertising enquiry and we will share a quote for your plan.

Who runs the screens inside the autos?

The auto screens run with our auto partners in Hyderabad. Commute Ads plans your campaign with you, reviews your ad, books it with our partners and shares photo proof once it is running.

What proof do I get that my auto ad ran?

We share photos from the autos showing your ad on the screen, so you can see it running.

Which areas of Hyderabad can my auto ad cover?

Tell us the areas you want to reach, such as a market, a college or the streets around your shop, and we will confirm what our partner autos can cover.

Can I run auto ads and cab ads together?

Yes. You can pair auto ads for local streets with in-cab advertising for office-goers and airport travellers. We plan both as one campaign.
