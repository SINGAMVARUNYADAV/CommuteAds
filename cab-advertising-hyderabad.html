What is in-cab advertising

A screen at eye level,
for the whole ride.

In-cab screen advertising places a locked-down tablet on the back of the front headrest, facing the passenger. Each screen runs a repeating 5-minute loop: about 3 minutes of infotainment and 2 minutes of ads in 15-second slots, so passengers keep watching and your brand is seen again and again. It is DOOH advertising (digital out-of-home) that happens inside the cab instead of beside the road.

Before your ad counts, the screen checks that someone is actually in the back seat. That means zero blind views: you pay for people, not empty cars. Passengers can't skip, mute or close the ad, and every ad carries a QR code that takes them straight to your offer.

In-cab advertising FAQs.

How much does cab advertising cost in Hyderabad?

Cost depends on how many cabs, which areas, which hours and how long your campaign runs. You pay only for verified views, plays recorded while a passenger is confirmed in the seat. Use the budget calculator in our media kit or pre-book for a quote.

Which cabs carry CommuteAds screens?

Our screens run in cabs that drive for Uber, Ola and Rapido, plus corporate and airport cabs, across Hyderabad. The network has 100 live cabs today.

How is in-cab advertising different from cab branding?

Cab branding puts a sticker or wrap on the outside of a cab for people on the road to see. In-cab advertising plays your video on a screen in front of the passenger for the whole ride, counts a view only when someone is in the seat, adds a QR code, and lets you change the ad any time.

What is DOOH advertising?

DOOH (digital out-of-home) advertising shows ads on digital screens outside the home, such as billboards, transit screens and in-cab screens. CommuteAds runs DOOH cab ads on screens inside Uber, Ola, Rapido and airport cabs in Hyderabad.

Are cab ads better than auto advertising?

They do different jobs. Auto ads and cab branding are static prints seen briefly by people on the road. In-cab screen ads play video to the passenger for the whole ride, count a view only when someone is in the seat, and carry a QR code, so you can measure results.

Can I target specific areas of Hyderabad?

Yes. Choose tech parks, neighbourhoods or the airport corridor, such as Hitec City, Gachibowli, the Financial District, Madhapur, Jubilee Hills and RGIA Airport, and the times of day your ad should run.

How quickly can my cab ad go live?

We review every creative for quality and compliance, typically within 24 to 48 hours. Once approved, your ad goes live across the fleet straight away.

What ad formats do you accept?

MP4 or MOV video in 1080p (16:9), in 10-second or 20-second formats, under 200 MB.
