Questions? Answers.

How quickly can my campaign go live?

After you submit your campaign through the dashboard, our team reviews the creative for quality and compliance, typically within 24–48 hours. Once approved, your ad goes live across the fleet straight away.

What video formats and specs are required?

MP4 or MOV in 1080p (16:9). Ads run in 10-second and 20-second formats, under 200 MB. Upload directly or share a hosted video link, and we'll verify everything before approval.

What is CPM pricing, and what's the minimum spend?

CPM (cost per mille) means you pay per 1,000 verified views: times a passenger was confirmed in the cab seat. In-cab rates range from ₹150 (Uber, Ola & Rapido) to ₹450 (airport transfers). The minimum daily budget is ₹1,000.

Can I pause or change my ad mid-campaign?

Yes. Edit your campaign or creative any time from the dashboard; a new creative is re-approved briefly before it plays. Pausing is immediate, and you aren't charged while paused.

How does passenger detection work?

Each cab screen checks that a passenger is actually present in the back seat. Your ad only counts as a view, and you're only charged, when a real passenger is confirmed. That is what makes zero blind views possible.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes. Every campaign gets a proper invoice from CommuteAds Private Limited (CIN U73100TS2026PTC223598), downloadable from your dashboard. For any billing question, email connect@commuteads.com.
