Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you launch.

After you submit your campaign through the dashboard, our team reviews the creative asset for quality and compliance — typically within 24–48 hours. Once approved, your ad goes live instantly across the entire fleet.

We accept MP4 or MOV files in 1080p (16:9 aspect ratio). Ads are available in 10-second and 20-second formats. File size should be under 200MB. You can upload directly or provide a hosted video URL. Our team will verify everything before approval.

CPM (Cost per Mille) means you pay per 1,000 verified impressions — times when a passenger was physically confirmed in the cab seat. Our rates range from ₹150 CPM (Uber, Ola & Rapido) to ₹450 CPM (airport transfers). The minimum daily budget is ₹1,000.

Yes. You can edit your campaign details or video creative at any time from the dashboard. When you save changes, the campaign enters Pending Review briefly while our team re-approves the new creative. Pausing is immediate and you won't be charged during paused periods.

Each cab screen checks that a passenger is actually present in the back seat. Your ad only counts as a view — and you are only charged — when a real passenger is confirmed as present. This is what makes our zero-blind-view guarantee possible.

Every campaign gets a proper invoice from CommuteAds Private Limited (CIN U73100TS2026PTC223598), downloadable from your dashboard (Billing tab). For GST invoicing, share your GSTIN in the pre-book form or email connect@commuteads.com and our team will confirm the details.
