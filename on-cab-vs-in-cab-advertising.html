On-Cab vs In-Cab
Advertising.

Cab advertising comes in two kinds. On-cab advertising brands the outside of the cab for people on the road. In-cab advertising plays your ad on a screen inside, in front of the passenger. Here is how they compare, and when each one makes sense.

Explore in-cab advertising Compare side by side
Two kinds of cab ads

Outside the cab,
or inside it.

On-cab advertising

Roof-top boards, wraps, door stickers and rear-window graphics. Your brand travels the city and is seen by drivers, riders and pedestrians for a few seconds at a time. Reach is estimated, and changing the design means a new print.

In-cab advertising

A screen on the back of the front headrest plays your video to the passenger for the whole ride, often 30–60 minutes. With Commute Ads, a view counts only when someone is in the seat, every play is logged, and a QR code lets the passenger act on your offer.

Which to choose

When each kind
of cab ad works.

Choose on-cab branding when you want your name seen around the city and don't need to measure who noticed it, such as a broad brand-awareness push.

Choose in-cab advertising when you want a real person's attention, a way for them to respond, and proof that your ad played. It suits real estate launches, clinics, gyms, restaurants, coaching centres and any offer a passenger can act on by scanning a QR code.

On-cab and in-cab advertising FAQs.

What is on-cab advertising?

On-cab advertising is branding on the outside of a cab: roof-top boards, full or partial wraps, door stickers and rear-window graphics. It is seen by people on the road as the cab drives past.

What is in-cab advertising?

In-cab advertising plays your ad on a screen inside the cab, facing the passenger. Commute Ads runs video ads on headrest screens in cabs across Hyderabad, counts a view only when a passenger is in the seat, and logs every play in a live dashboard.

Which is better, on-cab or in-cab advertising?

They do different jobs. On-cab branding gives broad road visibility for a few seconds at a time. In-cab screens give one passenger your ad for the whole ride, with a QR code and proof of every play. If you need measurable attention and leads, in-cab is the stronger choice.

Does Commute Ads sell on-cab branding?

No. Commute Ads runs in-cab screens in Hyderabad and ads on screens inside autos through our partners. We do not sell roof boards or wraps.

How is the cost different?

On-cab branding is usually priced per cab per month, whether or not anyone notices it. With Commute Ads in-cab screens you pay only for verified views: plays recorded while a passenger was confirmed in the seat.
