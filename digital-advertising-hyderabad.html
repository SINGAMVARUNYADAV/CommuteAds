Why digital out-of-home

Digital ads people
actually see.

Online ads get scrolled past and skipped. DOOH advertising, digital screens out of the home, meets people where they are: on the way to work, to college or to the airport. A screen inside a cab or auto has one viewer, for the whole trip.

Unlike printed hoardings, a digital ad can be changed any time, and with Commute Ads in-cab screens every play is logged and counted only when a passenger is in the seat. A QR code on the ad turns attention into leads you can count.

Digital advertising FAQs.

What are digital ads?

Digital ads are ads shown on screens: phones, computers and digital out-of-home (DOOH) screens such as in-cab tablets, digital billboards and transit screens. Unlike print, a digital ad can be changed quickly and its plays can be measured.

What is the best way to advertise in Hyderabad?

It depends on who you want to reach. For office-goers and airport travellers, in-cab screens work well. For people in your neighbourhood, auto ads and local screens. Many businesses combine screens with Instagram and Facebook ads. Tell us your goal and we will suggest a mix.

How much does advertising in Hyderabad cost?

It depends on the screens, areas, hours and how long your campaign runs. With in-cab screens you pay only for verified views. Pre-book or send an enquiry and we will share a quote.

What is DOOH advertising?

DOOH (digital out-of-home) advertising shows ads on digital screens outside the home, such as billboards, transit screens and in-cab screens. Commute Ads runs DOOH advertising across Hyderabad.

Can I run one campaign across several kinds of screens?

Yes. We plan one campaign across in-cab screens, auto screens and, as they open, billboards, hostel screens and bus screens, plus Meta ads if you want them.
