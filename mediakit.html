ABOUT

Advertising nobody can skip.

CommuteAds mounts digital screens at eye level in the back seat of cabs across Hyderabad — running through Hitec City, Gachibowli, and the airport corridor. Your ad plays for the full length of the ride. No skip button, no scrolling past it.

Every screen runs a repeating 5-minute content loop: mostly infotainment, with a fixed share reserved for ads — so passengers stay engaged, and brands get guaranteed, repeated exposure.

Unlike traditional Out-of-Home, every play is logged in real time — verified proof-of-play, not modeled reach.

What 20% SOV looks like

Each cab plays 96 ad slots an hour. At 20% SOV, roughly 1 in every 5 slots is your ad — about 19 plays per hour, per cab, across all 100 cabs simultaneously. The rest of the inventory runs other brands' campaigns.
